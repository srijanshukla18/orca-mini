# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in the Orca Mini project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and trade-offs without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's information.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.
- Keep project spaces focused and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Spam or other conduct inappropriate in a professional community

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces officially associated with Orca Mini. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings, or restrict participation when necessary.

For a sensitive conduct report, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/srijanshukla18/browser/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue.
